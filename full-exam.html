Exam instructions

Answer 57 English questions followed by 57 Math questions. All 114 questions are multiple-choice, including variants of the source form's grid-ins. This practice exam follows a fixed order. It does not adapt difficulty from your answers.

Use scratch paper or the built-in scratchpad, but no calculator, reference materials, or outside help. Select an answer, then lock it. Locked answers cannot be revisited. A selection that has not been locked is not saved as an answer.

The timer does not pause. Warnings appear with 10, 5, and 1 minute remaining. At zero, the exam ends automatically. You can return in this same browser to view and download your results. The PDF includes all assigned questions, choices, answers, explanations, and English passages.

Use keys 1-4 to select a choice when the question has focus. Text size, dark mode, fullscreen, and a private device-only scratchpad are available.
